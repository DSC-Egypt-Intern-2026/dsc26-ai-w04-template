COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 15 January 2026 between Horus Real Estate Investments S.A.E., of 12 Mohamed Mazhar Street, Zamalek, Cairo (the "Landlord") and Sakura Coffee Roasters LLC, of 40 Road 9, Maadi, Cairo (the "Tenant"), for the ground-floor unit at 22 Road 233, Degla, Maadi (the "Premises"). The Landlord lets and the Tenant takes the Premises on the following terms.

Clause 1. Definitions
In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise, "Business Day" means a day other than Friday, Saturday or an official public holiday in the Arab Republic of Egypt; "Confidential Information" has the meaning given in the confidentiality clause; and "Effective Date" means the date on which the last party signs this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Words in the singular include the plural and the other way round.

Clause 2. Term
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of three years from that date (the "Initial Term"), unless it is ended earlier in accordance with its provisions. The parties agree that the Initial Term has been chosen to allow the Tenant to recover its set-up costs, and that the duration of the Agreement shall not be shortened by any change in the volume of orders placed.

Clause 3. Renewal
At the end of the Initial Term this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of one year each, on the same terms, unless either party notifies the other in writing of its wish not to renew at least 120 days before the end of the current period. Any change to the prices on renewal must be agreed in writing; failing agreement, the prices of the expiring period continue to apply.

Clause 4. Rent and Payment Terms
the Tenant shall pay each undisputed invoice within 10 days of receiving it, by bank transfer to the account named on the invoice, in Egyptian pounds. Late payments carry interest at the Central Bank of Egypt's discount rate plus two per cent per year from the due date until the date of payment. A party that disputes an invoice in good faith shall pay the undisputed part and give reasons in writing.

Clause 5. Limitation of Liability
Except in the case of fraud, wilful misconduct or death or personal injury caused by negligence, the total aggregate liability of the Landlord arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total rent paid by the Tenant in the twelve months before the claim. Neither party shall be liable to the other for any loss of profit, loss of business, loss of data or any indirect or consequential loss, even if it was advised that such loss was possible.

Clause 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party not less than 180 days' written notice. Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty days of being asked to do so, or if the other party becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation or ceases to carry on business. Termination does not affect rights and obligations that accrued before it.

Clause 7. Force Majeure
Neither party is in breach of this Agreement nor liable for any delay in performing its obligations if the delay results from events beyond its reasonable control, including floods, fire, epidemics, war, governmental action or a general failure of the electricity or telecommunications networks. If such an event continues for more than sixty days, either party may end this Agreement by written notice.

Clause 8. Assignment
Neither party may assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in breach of this clause is void.

Clause 9. Notices
Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier or by email to the address of the receiving party set out at the head of this Agreement, or to another address that party has notified in writing. A notice delivered by email is deemed received on the next Business Day.

Clause 10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Cairo Economic Court to settle any dispute that cannot be resolved amicably within thirty days of one party notifying the other of it.

Clause 11. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all previous agreements, promises and understandings between them relating to its subject matter. No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both parties.

Signed in two originals, one for each party.
